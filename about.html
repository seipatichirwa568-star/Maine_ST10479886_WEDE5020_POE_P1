About the brand

Creative accessories with character

Skull Bracelets is a student website concept for a small accessory business offering skull-inspired bracelet designs.

Our mission

To make distinctive bracelet designs easier to discover and enquire about.

Our vision

To build a recognisable accessory brand that values creativity, quality and clear customer communication.

Our values

Creativity, customer service, honest product information, affordability and respect.

Our approach

We encourage customers to browse product details, see photographs and request confirmation before making purchases.

Frequently asked questions

Can I buy a bracelet directly on this website?

Not yet. This is a product-showcase and enquiry website, not an online checkout. Use the enquiry form to ask about products.

Are bracelet prices final?

The displayed prices are those listed in the original project. Stock, final pricing and delivery costs must be confirmed.

Can I ask about bulk orders?

Yes. Choose "Bulk or custom request" on the enquiry form and describe what you need.

Illustrative Johannesburg area map

The original project mentions Johannesburg, but does not provide a verified physical store address. The map illustrates the city centre, not an actual store or collection point.
